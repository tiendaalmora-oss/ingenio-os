# 00 - Vision

Ingenio OS is built to be a self-sustaining "Sales & Operational Machine" for a single operator.
The ultimate vision is a system where:
1.  Meta Ads drive traffic to highly optimized HTML landing pages.
2.  Leads click the WhatsApp button and are ingested via Webhooks.
3.  The AI Router instantly understands their needs and assigns them to the correct sales funnel (e.g., VerdeOS).
4.  The Gatekeeper AI handles objections, verifies scale compatibility, and closes the sale.
5.  The human operator only intervenes when the AI escalates to 'humano' status.
