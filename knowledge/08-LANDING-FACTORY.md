# 08 - Landing Factory

*   Pure HTML landing pages are favored for extreme performance on Meta Ads.
*   Stored in `landing-verdulero` and other specific folders.
*   **Optimization Rules**: 
    *   No heavy client-side frameworks for standard sales pages.
    *   Use `loading="lazy"` on heavy images.
    *   Use `preload="metadata"` and `playsinline` on videos.
    *   CSS must be highly optimized and minified.
